# Laundry Buddy — Stop Writing on Paper. Start Running a Real Business.

> The all-in-one order management system built for laundry shops in the Philippines.

---

## The Problem

Walk into almost any laundry shop in the Philippines today and you'll find the same thing: a worn notebook, a whiteboard covered in erased names, and a group chat where half the orders get buried under memes.

**The reality of running a laundry shop without a system:**

- Orders get lost between shifts when staff forget to hand off notes
- Customers call to ask "where's my laundry?" and nobody knows the answer
- Rush fees, starch charges, and add-ons get forgotten at checkout
- No way to know which orders are overdue or sitting untouched for hours
- End-of-month reporting means manually counting receipts

This isn't just frustrating — it's money left on the table and customers lost to better-run competitors.

---

## The Solution

**Laundry Buddy** is a real-time, web-based operations dashboard that brings every order, every staff member, and every peso under one roof.

No more notebooks. No more lost orders. No more guessing.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Real-Time Kanban Board — See Every Order, All at Once

Your entire operation on one screen. Orders flow through 7 clearly defined stages:

**New Order → For Pickup → Arrived at Shop → Processing → Ready for Delivery → Out for Delivery → Completed**

- **Drag and drop** orders between stages — no typing, no clicking through menus
- **Live updates** across all devices — move a card on the counter tablet, and the delivery rider's phone updates instantly
- **Color-coded age indicators** — green means fresh, orange means it's been a while, red means a customer is about to call
- **New order alerts** with browser notifications — staff get notified even when the tab isn't active
- **Pulsing highlight** on new cards so nothing gets missed during a busy shift

### 2. Order Lifecycle Management — Every Detail Captured

Create an order in under 30 seconds with everything that matters:

- **Customer name, contact number, address**
- **Order type**: Walk-in or Pickup — the system auto-sets the starting status so nothing falls through the cracks
- **Philippine phone number validation** (09XXXXXXXXX format) — no more invalid numbers in your records
- **Weight, price per kg, total price** — auto-calculated on creation
- **Notes** for special instructions (allergies to detergent, fabric sensitivities, etc.)

Orders are never truly deleted — soft deletes keep your history intact for audits and disputes.

### 3. Smart Pricing — No More Manual Math

Set your price per kg once. The system does the rest.

- **Price snapshot on order creation** — if you raise prices tomorrow, old orders stay at the rate they were promised
- **Additional Charges Catalog** — create reusable charges (Rush Fee, Starch, Fabric Softener, Dry Clean) and apply them at order creation
- **Charges are snapshotted** — applied charges are locked to the order, so edits to the catalog never corrupt existing orders

### 4. Delivery Board — Purpose-Built for Your Riders

A dedicated delivery view separate from the main board:

- Two-column layout: **Ready for Delivery** and **Out for Delivery**
- One tap to mark an order "Out for Delivery" or "Completed"
- **Bulk actions** — send all ready orders out at once, or complete a full delivery run in one click
- Customer address and contact number front-and-center so riders never have to dig

### 5. Reports & Analytics — Know Your Numbers

Stop guessing how much you made last month.

- **Revenue, total weight, order count** — all filterable by date range, status, and order type
- **Daily trend charts** — see your peak days and slow periods at a glance
- **Status distribution pie chart** — identify bottlenecks in your workflow
- **Completion rate and average order value** — KPIs that actually tell you if the business is healthy
- **One-click PDF export** with your shop's branding for record-keeping or sharing with partners

### 6. Role-Based Access — The Right People See the Right Things

Two roles, each with exactly what they need:

| Role | What They Can Do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Admin** | Configure pricing, manage team, update branding, view reports |
| **Staff** | Create and manage orders, move cards on the board |

Staff can't touch pricing. Admins control the shop configuration.

### 7. Branding & Team Management — It Feels Like Your Business

- Upload your **shop logo** and set your **shop name** — appears across the dashboard and exported PDFs
- **Invite staff by email** — they receive a default password and can change it after first login
- Team members listed with role and join date — always know who has access

### 9. Security You Can Trust

- **Row-level security** on every database table — your shop's data is yours alone
- **Soft deletes** — nothing is ever permanently gone without explicit action
- **Password policies** — minimum 8 characters, enforced on set and change
